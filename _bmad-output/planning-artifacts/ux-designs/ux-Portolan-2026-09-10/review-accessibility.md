# Accessibility — Portolan

Reviewer lens: **does the Accessibility Floor tell the truth?** Not "is Portolan accessible" — the scoping decisions (no keyboard traversal, no screen-reader support) were taken knowingly and are not reopened here. Every ratio and every colour-distance below was recomputed from the hex values in `DESIGN.md`'s frontmatter (sRGB relative luminance per WCAG 2.x; dichromat simulation per Viénot, Brettel & Mollon 1999; ΔE in CIELAB).

## Verdict

The floor is **honest about what it refuses and dishonest about the one thing it claims to have solved.** Keyboard traversal and screen-reader support are excluded in plain, unsoftened language in a place a reader will find — that half is exemplary. But the bullet **"Value-level colour is colour-vision-safe"** is true for two of the four pastille families and false for the other two, verified numerically: the health fix was generalised into a blanket claim, and the obligation it delegates to `DESIGN.md` is never discharged there. Alongside it sit three smaller promises the documents contradict from inside themselves — the contrast table measures a colour the components section does not assign, the 9px type floor is broken by four roles in its own ramp at the default setting, and "nothing drifts" is refuted by a `drift: ±3.4px` token in the other spine.

The measurement discipline is real where it was applied: the iso-luminant zone rotation genuinely works, and nine of the twelve table rows check out. The problem is coverage and one generalisation, not sloppiness.

## Overclaims (critical)

**1. "Value-level colour is colour-vision-safe" — true for health and type, false for stack and network.**

This is the one the brief warned about, and it happened exactly as predicted: the health fix was allowed to imply the other three families were covered. `.memlog` records the moment. Entry 101 corrects the earlier false claim and instructs: *"EXPERIENCE.md's Accessibility Floor must state plainly that value-level distinctions are not covered."* Entry 112 then fixes health alone and licenses the generalisation: *"EXPERIENCE.md's Accessibility Floor can now state that value-level colour distinctions are colour-vision-safe."* One family was repaired; four families' worth of claim was written.

Measured, deuteranopia, dark palette:

| Family | Shape | Values | Worst pair | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Object type | square | 5 | ΔE 20.5 | safe |
| Health | circle | 3 | ΔE 18.7 | safe |
| **Stack** | diamond | 7 | **ΔE 3.9** (`stack-3` `#6FB2A0` / `stack-4` `#C48FA8`) | **not distinguishable** |
| **Network** | hexagon | 6 | **ΔE 2.6** (`network-1` `#4A8296` / `network-3` `#7A7695`) | **not distinguishable** |

Light palette is worse: stack ΔE 2.9, and zone tints `t1`/`t3` simulate to **ΔE 0.0** — byte-identical after deuteranopia.

The concrete downstream loss is nameable. Flow 1 step 6, "**Climax — the intruder**", is *"a bubble sitting inside the `frontend` zone whose stack pastille says something else. The mismatch between the zone and the badge* is *the anomaly."* Both halves of that comparison — zone tint and stack pastille — are in the failing set. A deuteranope cannot perform the second of the primary journey's three discoveries. Nothing in either spine says so.

*Fix (wording, not palette):* replace the bullet with a split claim. In scope: "Family is shape-coded, so the dimension being read never depends on colour. **Two families are value-safe under deuteranopia and protanopia: object type — which is the only family at rung 0, and therefore the whole of the screenshot frame — and health, for which blue/amber/red replaced green/amber/red precisely to achieve this.**" Then, under *Explicitly out of scope, v1*, a third bullet in the same register as the other two: "**Value-level colour in the stack and network pastille families.** Six-value rotations at pastille size are not separable under deuteranopia or protanopia; the exact value is available only in the detail panel. Raised and not solved in v1. Stated plainly rather than softened. **Consequence, stated: the zone-versus-stack-pastille mismatch that Flow 1 step 6 turns on is not available to a colour-blind reader.**" And delete "`DESIGN.md` owns keeping it true" unless `DESIGN.md` is given a matching constraint — an unowned obligation reads as coverage.

**2. The contrast table's headline row measures a colour the components section does not assign.**

Row 1 — *"Identifier on bubble body | **15.0** / 18.1 | 7:1 — this is the channel `pgdata` vs `pg-data` is read on"* — is the highest-stakes row in the file. But Components → Bubble reads: *"identifier beneath in `{typography.bubble-id}` at `{colors.ink-3}`"*. `#5D6A74` on `body-mid` `#0B1116` is **3.42:1**, and **4.74:1** in light. That is below 4.5:1 in the dark palette, on 9px type, against a self-declared 7:1 floor — a 4.4× gap between the asserted number and the specified one.

The 15.0 figure is `{colors.ink}` on the body (14.98 — correct arithmetic for the wrong token). `{colors.ink}` is presumably the *name*'s colour, which Components → Bubble never states. So the row is either measuring an unassigned token or contradicting an assigned one, and one of the two is a real 3.42:1 failure. This is the single row a builder is most likely to trust without rechecking.

*Fix:* decide which colour the identifier takes, assign it in Components → Bubble, and re-measure the row against that token. If the intent is the instrument-grade quiet of `ink-3`, say so and drop the 7:1 claim; if the intent is 15:1, `ink-3` is the wrong token for `bubble-id`.

## Exclusions — stated plainly enough?

**Keyboard traversal of the graph — yes.** Own bullet, own heading (*Explicitly out of scope, v1*), no hedging, and it draws the further consequence out loud ("the map is **mouse / trackpad only** in v1"). This is what an honest narrow floor looks like.

**Screen-reader support — yes.** Same treatment. "There is no accessible text equivalent of the map in v1" leaves nothing to infer.

**Value-level colour dependence — no.** Not merely under-stated: stated as *solved*. See Overclaim 1.

**Placement — half-covered.** The Accessibility Floor sits in `EXPERIENCE.md` at section 8 of 11, before Responsive & Platform. A reader of that spine will meet it. But **`DESIGN.md` has no accessibility section, no floor, and no cross-reference to one** — and `DESIGN.md` is the document a visual builder opens, the document that owns every colour, and the document `EXPERIENCE.md` delegates the CVD obligation to. Its Colors section opens *"Measured, not asserted"*, which reads as full coverage of a topic it covers partially. Someone implementing the palette from `DESIGN.md` alone will never learn that keyboard and screen-reader are out, nor that colour carries an unresolved value-level dependence.

*Fix:* a four-line block at the end of `DESIGN.md`'s Colors section — "Colour-vision coverage: type ✓, health ✓, stack ✗, network ✗; zone tint is a grouping cue only, identity is the label. Keyboard and screen-reader support are out of scope for v1 — see `EXPERIENCE.md` → Accessibility Floor." One paragraph closes the gap.

## Holes never decided either way

**1. Whether ordinary tab-focus exists at all.** This is the big one, and the session appears never to have reached it.

What *was* decided: no keyboard **accelerators** (`.memlog` 86 — "Chosen WITHOUT the keyboard-accelerator variant"; derived consequences are about `Esc` and `0`), and no keyboard **traversal of the graph**. Both are about the map.

What the spines actually say: *"**Keyboard shortcuts — none, anywhere.**"* — under a heading called **Banned**. "Anywhere" is broader than the map, and "Banned" is stronger than "out of scope". Then, in `DESIGN.md`'s Do's and Don'ts: *"Use glass for hover, **focus**, accent or emphasis"* — a Don't. That is the only occurrence of the word "focus" in either spine, and no replacement treatment is defined. There is no focus token, no focus ring, no outline spec, no statement that the left menu's checkboxes, the three bottom tabs, the toolbar chips or the Export row are reachable by Tab.

So a builder reading both spines finds: keyboard input banned "anywhere", the one obvious focus treatment forbidden, and nothing else. The reasonable inference is that the entire chrome is mouse-only — which would be a vastly larger exclusion than the two that were actually taken, and would put a read-only product with a left menu, three tabs and five controls out of reach of anyone who cannot use a pointer. It may also be an accident of phrasing. **The spines do not settle it, and it needs one sentence, not a discussion.**

*Fix:* in *Banned*, narrow the wording to what was decided — "**Keyboard accelerators — none.** No single-key shortcuts, no modifier chords, no `Esc`, no `0`." Then, in the Accessibility Floor's *In scope* list, add: "**Standard tab focus on the chrome.** The left menu, the bottom tabs and the on-screen controls are ordinary focusable elements in DOM order with a visible focus indicator. Only the *graph canvas* is pointer-only. The no-accelerators decision governs shortcuts, not focusability." And `DESIGN.md` needs a focus token — the Don't correctly reserves glass for selection, so focus needs its own mark (a 2px `{colors.steel}` outline at 2px offset would be consistent with the metal discipline and reads at 5.3:1 on the panel). If the opposite is intended, say *that* plainly instead — but it must be said.

**2. Whether type is in screen space or world space under the LOD ladder.** `DESIGN.md` fixes bubble radii "at `{density.scale}` 1.00, **LOD rung 0**" and separately observes that 25% is *"the size a bubble actually is on a 300-object map"*. If type scales with the canvas transform, a 9.5px zone label renders at 2.4px on the frame that is both the landing state and the screenshot. If it does not, the 9px floor and "drop the LOD rung instead" are coherent — but nobody has written down which it is. The whole type floor rests on this and neither spine names it.

**3. Browser and OS zoom.** Never mentioned in either spine, and actively blocked by a decision that was taken for other reasons. `{layout.min-width}` 1440px with an off-chart refusal below it means the standard remedy for small text fails: a 1440px display trips off-chart at **110%** browser zoom; 1920px at 150%; even 2560px at 200%. Combined with an in-app ceiling of 1.15×, there is **no route to 200% text anywhere in the product**, and no route to the 16px browser default (sans tops out at 14.95px). The 1440px floor is a legitimate scoping decision; its interaction with the *stated* adjustable-text requirement was never noticed.

**4. Whether the layout survives `{typography.scale}` 1.15.** The left menu is fixed at 236px and the detail panel at 320px; type multiplies, columns do not. No overflow, wrap, scroll or truncation behaviour is specified for either.

**5. A minimum mark size, to match the 9px minimum type size.** There is a hard floor for glyphs and none for pastilles, whose 10×10px is the sole carrier of the health verdict. This becomes live immediately: the rail is `n × 10px + (n−1) × 5.5px`, and the core it must sit inside is `0.59 × 2r`. Every container carries at least type + stack + one network + health = **56.5px of rail into a 54.3px core** at default density; a volume needs 41px into 37.8px. Something has to give, and the three candidates are shrinking the marks (no floor stated), shrinking the gap, or breaching the core (forbidden, and "the straight pastille rail depends on it"). The spines forbid one option and are silent on the other two.

**6. Hover / pointer affordance.** "No hover state" appears twice, both times as a prohibition on using *glass* for hover. No hover treatment is defined anywhere else, so whether a bubble indicates it is clickable before you click it is undecided. Relevant because it is the only remaining discovery channel once keyboard is out.

## Findings

- **[critical]** "Value-level colour is colour-vision-safe" is false for the stack (ΔE 2.9 deut.) and network (ΔE 2.6 deut.) families; the health fix is generalised into a blanket claim, and the obligation delegated to `DESIGN.md` is never discharged there (`EXPERIENCE.md` → Accessibility Floor → In scope, bullet 2). *Fix:* split the claim — name type and health as safe, and add stack/network value-level colour as a third **Explicitly out of scope** bullet, with the Flow 1 step 6 consequence stated.
- **[critical]** Contrast table row 1 asserts 15.0:1 for the bubble identifier at a 7:1 floor; the token Components → Bubble actually assigns it (`{colors.ink-3}`) measures **3.42:1** dark / 4.74:1 light — below 4.5:1, on 9px type (`DESIGN.md` → Colors → contrast table, and → Components → Bubble). *Fix:* assign the identifier colour explicitly and re-measure the row against it; `ink-3` and a 7:1 floor cannot both stand.
- **[high]** The network pastille is offered as the exact answer once zone tints repeat past six networks, but `pastille-network-*` is the same six-step rotation — network 7's hexagon is network 1's hexagon (`DESIGN.md` → Colors, "Beyond six networks…"; `EXPERIENCE.md` → Network zone). The remedy is the thing it remedies. It is also absent at rung 0 and CVD-confusable. *Fix:* state the real answer — beyond six networks, exact membership is a detail-panel fact, not a map fact — rather than pointing at a channel with the same repeat.
- **[high]** The contrast table covers nine map-mark combinations and none of the five text roles carrying an explicit colour token; **three of those five fail 4.5:1**: `bubble-id` at `ink-3` **3.42** / 4.74, detail-panel keys and left-menu counts at `ink-3` **3.51** / 3.84, node-backdrop labels at `#46535C` **2.49–2.54** dark (`DESIGN.md` → Components: Bubble, Detail panel, Left menu, Node backdrop). Node names are what Flow 3 is read on. *Fix:* add a chrome-text block to the table and lift `ink-3` where it carries text — `ink-2` `#94A3AD` gives 7.52:1 on the panel at no cost to the register.
- **[high]** The "absolute floor after multiplication is 9px — no glyph… at any scale, at any zoom" is contradicted by the ramp in the same file's frontmatter: four roles are below it at scale **1.00** (`section-label` 8, `graduation` 8, `zone-sub` 8.5, `marginalia` 8.5) and six at 0.90 (down to 7.2px). The floor holds only at 1.15 (`DESIGN.md` → Typography; frontmatter `typography.*`). Typography also praises "a 8px label" four paragraphs earlier. *Fix:* either restate the floor as what it evidently is — "9px for map labels; chart marginalia and section heads run to 7.2px" — or raise the four roles.
- **[high]** "Motion is local deformation only, never translation… the centre does not move by a pixel… **Nothing drifts**" is refuted by `{motion.breathe}`'s own `drift: ±3.4px` / `drift-periods 9–23s` and by `.memlog` 148 ("core, name, id and pastille rail drift") (`EXPERIENCE.md` → State Patterns → Alive at rest, vs `DESIGN.md` frontmatter `motion.breathe`). Every label and every click target travels up to 6.8px peak-to-peak, continuously, by default — and there is no keyboard alternative for anyone who cannot track it. *Fix:* say what `DESIGN.md` actually specifies — "the **layout anchor** never moves: a bubble is always found where it was left. The drawn body excursions within its reserved cell by ±3.4px, zero-mean and non-accumulating; `prefers-reduced-motion` stills that excursion with the rest of `{motion.breathe}`." Drop "Nothing drifts."
- **[high]** No route to enlarged text: the 1440px off-chart refusal trips at 110% browser zoom on a 1440px display and at 200% on a 2560px one, while `{typography.scale}` caps at 1.15× (sans 14.95px, below the 16px browser default). Neither spine mentions browser or OS zoom (`EXPERIENCE.md` → Responsive & Platform / Accessibility Floor; `DESIGN.md` → Layout). *Fix:* state it in the Floor — "Adjustable text size spans 0.90–1.15 and multiplies every rendered size. Browser zoom is **not** a supported enlargement path: it reduces the CSS viewport below `{layout.min-width}` and triggers the off-chart message. In-app text scaling is the only route, and its ceiling is +15%." An honest ceiling is a promise; an unbounded one is not.
- **[medium]** Zone isolines fail their own 3:1 floor as rendered: `{opacity.zone-isoline}` **0.85** composites 3.22–3.67 down to **2.68–3.03**, failing on five of six tints (`DESIGN.md` → contrast table row 9, vs frontmatter `opacity.zone-isoline`). *Fix:* measure the composited mark, or drop the opacity — it buys little against a 1.18:1 field.
- **[medium]** Bubble contour is measured over the canvas (3.25 / 3.04) but bubbles sit inside zones by construction; over `zone-tint-1` it is **2.75:1**, below the stated 3:1 (`DESIGN.md` → contrast table row 11). Same class of error the edge rows got right. *Fix:* measure the contour over all six tints, as the edges already are.
- **[medium]** Focus indication is forbidden without a replacement: the only mention of "focus" in either spine is `DESIGN.md`'s Don't reserving glass for selection; no focus token, ring or outline exists (`DESIGN.md` → Do's and Don'ts, row 5). *Fix:* add a focus token distinct from glass — e.g. 2px `{colors.steel}` at 2px offset, 5.3:1 on the panel — and pair it with the tab-focus sentence in the hole above.
- **[medium]** The layered first draw is kept under `prefers-reduced-motion` on an "explanatory motion kept" rationale, but the spines describe it as decoration: *"The first movement is **the pitch** — he has not clicked anything yet"* (Flow 2 step 2). It explains no change. `{motion.relayout}` — 900ms of every body travelling curved paths across the whole map — is also kept, and large-area movement is the hazard class the media query primarily exists for (`EXPERIENCE.md` → State Patterns → Reduced motion; `DESIGN.md` `motion.draw` / `motion.relayout`). The line is defensible; the kept set is not the small half. *Fix:* apply the document's own test — still `{motion.draw}` and `{motion.settle}` under the media query (render the finished map directly; nothing is lost, as `motion.reduced-motion` already says of breathe), keep `{motion.enter}` / `{motion.exit}` / selection, and note that `{motion.relayout}` is retained deliberately because following an object between geometries *is* the function, with its 900ms amplitude stated.

- **[medium]** No minimum mark size to match the 9px type floor, and the rail already overflows the core: 4 marks = 56.5px into a 54.3px container core at default density; a 3-mark volume needs 41px into 37.8px (`DESIGN.md` → Shapes → `shape.pastille`, `shape.bubble.core`, `shape.bubble.radius`). - **[medium]** The adjustable-text promise is stated with no range in the spine that makes it a floor item, and neither spine says the layout survives the top of the range against fixed 236px / 320px columns (`EXPERIENCE.md` → Accessibility Floor, bullet 3). *Fix:* carry the 0.90/1.00/1.15 range and "multiplies every rendered size; density is a separate control" into the Floor bullet, and add one sentence on chrome behaviour at 1.15.
