# Reconciliation — EXPERIENCE.md against the PRD

**Input:** `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-Portolan-2026-09-10/EXPERIENCE.md` (418 lines, status `final`).
**Target:** `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-Portolan-2026-09-11/prd.md` (FR-1..FR-63, NFR-1..NFR-18).

Method: every behavioural statement in EXPERIENCE.md was walked section by section and matched
against the PRD's 63 FRs, 18 NFRs, §1 Vision, §5 exclusions and §6 open questions. Absences were
confirmed by literal search of `prd.md`.

Severity key:
- **Critical** — a signature behaviour or a journey climax that an implementer cannot infer from the PRD; the product is a different product without it.
- **High** — a normative rule or figure with consequences an implementer would get wrong by default.
- **Medium** — a real decision lost; recoverable from DESIGN.md or by asking, but not present.
- **Low** — colour, rationale or placement detail; loss is tolerable.

## E. Summary of what to fix, in order

1. **Write the missing FRs.** Minimum set, in descending severity: continuous breathing with the
   `0px` translation invariant; node-region proportionality; the orphan bubble; pastille rail
   capacity (5/4/3, 6/5/4, 8px floor, drop network badges from the right); the octave pattern on the
   zone field; the control-chip vocabulary with *no chip is ever a filled button*; the toolbar as a
   surface; *Fit to chart*; the pan/zoom gesture set including `Space` + drag; the chart legend as a
   permanent on-screen band; detail-panel loading with no spinner; the voice rule (both halves,
   plus the microcopy constraints); the stable unique silhouette; protrusion adjacency and mono.
2. **Resolve the two live conflicts.** FR-23's one-hop default against Flow 1 step 7; NFR-9's
   reopening of the screen-space ruling against NFR-10's rendered-size floors.
3. **Repair §6.3's over-generalisation** — zone labels *may* move where a clear position exists;
   contours may not. And re-cite: FR-16 is not the relevant requirement.
4. **Restore the `[ASSUMPTION]` flags** on screen-space marks, the legend's permanence, the hover
   affordance and *Safe to share*'s default, and the **[DEPARTS FROM BRIEF]** flags on health
   framing, pastille encoding, palette, export and zoom-vs-filter.
5. **Add a journeys section.** The three flows are not decoration: the discovery-per-rung ladder,
   the escalating selection arc, and the eight failure paths are requirements the FR form cannot
   express, and EXPERIENCE's coverage audit is the only check that every surface is reachable.
6. **Fix the four dangling references** so the PRD stops citing rules it does not contain.
