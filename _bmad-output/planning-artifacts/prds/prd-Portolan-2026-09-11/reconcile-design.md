# Reconciliation — DESIGN.md → prd.md

**What this checks.** DESIGN.md (1258 lines, `status: final`) is the visual spine. The PRD was
assembled from a subagent's EXTRACT of it. This file records what the extract-and-assemble pass
lost, contradicted, or left unresolved.

**What this deliberately does not check.** Token values, hex codes, pixel geometry, stroke widths,
font sizes, layer stacking, motion easings and per-component appearance are DESIGN.md's to own and
correctly absent from a PRD. A missing token is reported below **only** where the omission drops a
product-level commitment, a floor, a guarantee, a named control, or the reason a choice was made.

**Severity key.** `CRITICAL` — a stated floor, guarantee or shipped capability is missing or
contradicted, and building from the PRD alone would produce the wrong product. `HIGH` — a
product-constraining decision or a resolved question is absent, or the PRD contradicts DESIGN.md.
`MEDIUM` — rationale, qualitative intent or a secondary rule lost; recoverable by reading
DESIGN.md but not visible to anyone who does not. `LOW` — register, provenance or wording.

---

## 1. Headline findings

1. **The whole brand register is gone.** `cartographie assumée`, the 14th-century chart, the
   precision-instrument execution — none of it reaches the PRD. §1 Vision is functional prose only.
2. **The network octave pattern is missing as a requirement** and survives in the PRD only as an
   open question. NFR-7 requires 11 networks; nothing in the PRD says how more than six are told
   apart, and §6.1 therefore states the network-channel failure without the mitigation DESIGN.md
   adopted for it.
3. **Three decided floors became open questions or vanished:** the 6px attachment-stub minimum
   (decided in DESIGN.md, an open question in NFR-9), the 8×8px pastille mark floor (absent), the
   +32% deformation cap (absent, though NFR-9 asks whether it varies).
4. **The map breathes, and the PRD never says so** — nor carries the zero-translation contract
   (*amplitude bounds the outline, never the position*; labels and click targets never travel),
   which is the hardest guarantee in DESIGN.md.
5. **The seeded silhouette channel is gone.** FR-13 carries only the data channel (stretch). The
   recognition channel — contour seeded from the Docker ID, stable across every survey, identical in
   every screenshot — is unrepresented, and with it the two-channel finding that is the core of
   *Shapes*.
6. **No departure or assumption register.** DESIGN.md's five `[DEPARTS FROM BRIEF]` and eleven
   `[ASSUMPTION]` markers survive only where the PRD happens to restate their content. One of them
   inverts: §1 restates the brief's *"Portolan does not tell you something is wrong"* verbatim,
   which is the exact line DESIGN.md flags itself as departing from.

## 4. Suggested remedies, in priority order

1. **Add an FR for the network octave pattern** (hue = rotation index held for the life of the
   network; pattern = octave; the pattern rides the zone field so it survives rung 0; four patterns
   carry 24 networks against NFR-7's 11). Then revise §6.1, which currently reports the network
   failure with its mitigation removed.
2. **Add the mark floor and the rail-fit rule** — 8×8px minimum; shrink uniformly to the floor; drop
   network badges from the right; never wrap, never breach the core. FR-25 already depends on it.
3. **Add an FR for continuous motion** — that the map breathes, and the zero-translation contract:
   amplitude bounds the outline, never the position; labels and click targets never travel.
4. **Restore the 6px attachment-stub floor** as a requirement (NFR-9 keeps only the question of
   whether it is achievable at landing zoom).
5. **Add the seeded silhouette channel** to FR-13, with the two-channel distinction and the +32% /
   ±38° figures, plus the invariant core it may never breach.
6. **Add a brand/register paragraph to §1** — `cartographie assumée`, the precision instrument, the
   metrological reading, and the anti-brief (no texture, no parchment, no romance).
7. **Add the position-ownership rule** (zone owns, outline derives, no second mark may own position —
   the node-backdrop precedent) and fix §6.3's citation.
8. **Resolve the health departure** — carry it, or say why it was retired — and add the detail panel's
   permanent footer, which is the product's own answer to it.
9. **Add the missing named surfaces and controls** — the toolbar, *Fit to chart*, the on-screen chart
   legend band, the fixed three-column layout with its 884px canvas minimum.
10. **Add the two floors buried in prose** — staleness at fifteen minutes with text never below 4.2:1
    under veil, and the two dim levels that must stay visibly distinct.
11. **Add a departures-and-assumptions register** to the PRD, listing DESIGN.md's five
    `[DEPARTS FROM BRIEF]` and eleven `[ASSUMPTION]` markers with a resolution for each.
12. **Reconcile FR-51 with DESIGN.md** (contradiction C1) in whichever direction Jules rules.
