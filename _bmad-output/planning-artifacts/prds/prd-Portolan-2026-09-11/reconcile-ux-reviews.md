## Method, and the one thing that makes this hard

The UX memlog records closure **by count, not by finding**: entry 182 says "DESIGN.md fix pass
applied: 25 of 26 items", entry 186 says "EXPERIENCE.md fix pass applied: all 18 items". That is
~44 fix items against 78 findings, and nowhere does the memlog say which fix covers which finding —
the PRD memlog already flagged this (entry 17: *"la cloture par finding n'est pas verifiable"*).

So **"no recorded ruling"** below means: no `(decision)`/`(override)` entry and no *named* fix item
in the UX memlog. Findings that are pure document hygiene inside the spines (token plumbing, table
rows, duplicated prose, stale citations, orphan working files, rounding) are counted as legitimately
outside a PRD and are not listed as losses; they are summarised at the end.

**The PRD extract did keep a list of unruled findings.** PRD memlog entry 19 enumerates nine
"FINDINGS SANS ARBITRAGE ENREGISTRE" and the PRD answers all nine (six via the excluded set, plus
pastille-scale → NFR-9, frame rate → NFR-8, hover → FR-27). Every loss below is something **that
list itself missed** — which is the shape of the extract-and-assemble failure: the extract arbitrated
well on the findings it carried forward, and carried forward a subset it never verified as complete.

Items the PRD already handles and that are excluded by instruction from being re-reported: hop
default, in-product motion stop, text-size ceiling, light-palette deuteranopia defect plus the
accepted stack/network badge residual, chart legend decoding its own chart, search including image
tags, screen-side masking, no-auth-as-deployment-constraint.
