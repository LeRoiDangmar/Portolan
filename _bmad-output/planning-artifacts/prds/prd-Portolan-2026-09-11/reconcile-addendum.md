# Reconciliation — addendum (2026-09-09/10) against PRD (2026-09-11)

The PRD was assembled from a subagent's extract of the addendum rather than from the addendum
itself. This is a section-by-section pass over the addendum asking of every item: **carried** into
the PRD, **depth** (legitimately left behind — the addendum exists to hold what the PRD should not
carry), or **lost** (it should have surfaced and did not).

Section numbers below refer to the PRD as of 2026-09-11 after the insertion of §6.4 *The state of
the upstream documents* (which renumbered the standing counter-arguments to §6.5).

**Severity scale.** `HIGH` — a downstream reader (architecture, stories, README) will make a wrong
or unsupported decision. `MED` — a decision survives but its reason does not, so it can be
re-litigated or quietly reversed. `LOW` — depth correctly left behind, noted for completeness.
`OK` — carried faithfully, no action.

## Headline

Three things went wrong systematically, and they are the same three things:

1. **Rationales were dropped while their decisions were kept** (licence, 3D, images-not-nodes,
   dashboard exclusion). The PRD reads as a set of assertions where the addendum had arguments.
2. **Qualified claims were universalised.** The addendum is careful about what the competition does
   and does not render; §1 of the PRD is not.
3. **The addendum was orphaned.** The PRD never cites it, and the newly added §6.4 — which exists
   precisely to tell architecture which upstream documents to read — names `DESIGN.md` and
   `EXPERIENCE.md` and omits both `brief.md` and `addendum.md`. Every `LOW`/depth row below is only
   legitimately depth if someone downstream knows where to find it. As written, nobody does.

**The open-questions check passed.** All three addendum questions marked *binding input to
architecture* reached the PRD: Q1 as NFR-2 + NFR-3 + §6.2 rows 1–2 (strengthened, with the `:ro`
trap named), Q2 resolved into FR-7 (legitimately — the UX decided it), Q3 as §6.1 + NFR-7 (deepened,
with "not a three-container lab" verbatim). Consequences are intact in every case. The losses around
them are their *rationale* and one *discarded alternative*, not the questions themselves.

**The two previously corrected errors have NOT been reintroduced.** Verified by search: the PRD
nowhere claims Weave Scope was "the only tool that ever rendered a relational graph" (see B2 for a
drift in that direction that is not yet the error), and the PRD states no licence for any
third-party tool at all, so Netdata's GPLv3+ is not misdescribed. Also verified absent: the
do-not-use "2.5% market share" figure, Portainer's cumulative 30,000, Enlyft's 11,700 and the
April-2022 Mirantis figures.

## What to do

Ordered by cost-to-benefit, not by severity:

1. **§6.4 — add `addendum.md` (and `brief.md`) to the list of upstream documents architecture must
   read.** One line. Fixes E1 and de-risks every `LOW`/depth row.
2. **FR-1 or NFR-1 — state a Docker Engine / API version floor, and record the v29 caveats** (C4):
   nftables cannot be enabled in Swarm mode, and moby #51491 broke Swarm DNS. This is the one
   dropped item that is purely technical and will otherwise cost rework.
3. **NFR-17 — give the licence its reason** (B8): one sentence on the hosted-service risk, one on
   why permissive and BSL were both rejected, and the Grafana/Netdata precedent.
4. **§1 — restore the Netdata distinction and requalify the competitive claim** (B2, B3): "flows,
   not membership" for Netdata, and "the tools people actually run have no relational graph" in
   place of "none of them". This also permanently forecloses the already-corrected error.
5. **§6.1 — add Weave Scope #1636** (D8), and one clause in §1 or §6.1 saying who Weave Scope was
   and that it is dead (B4). Calibrates "the grave is genuinely avoided".
6. **§6.5 bullet 2 — restore the two balancing facts** (C1, C2): Swarm mode is not deprecated and is
   actively maintained; the "Mirantis will kill it" claim is rumour and contradicted by the pledge.
7. **§1 — restore the nine-year demand evidence** (B5) in one clause with the issue numbers, and
   "the incumbent will not build this" (B6). Cheap, and it is the PRD's only external evidence.
8. **FR-7 / §5 — decide the "group by image" view explicitly** (D6): either out of scope with a
   reason, or an open question. It should not simply be missing.
9. **§5 — point the 3D exclusion at its evidence** (B7) and mark it settled, not preferred.
